Depthworks support

Questions about your digging adventure? Find the essentials below or get in touch with FindusLab.

Contact support ↗

You can also email findus.lab@gmail.com directly.

01

My progress and settings

Your expedition saves on your device. There is no in-game account or cloud sync. Clearing the app's data or your browser's site data removes local saves; keep this in mind when changing devices.

02

Starting a fresh expedition

Open Settings → Reset Game to start again. Resetting your expedition keeps preferences such as sound, music, haptics, and camera shake.

03

Optional digging boosts

On Android and iOS, you can choose to watch a rewarded ad for a temporary boost. The game is playable without watching ads. An ad may be unavailable because of your connection, privacy choices, or ad availability.

04

Your privacy choices

When available, Settings → Ad privacy options lets you revisit your advertising consent. Read our privacy policy for information about local saves, advertising, and your rights.

Something not working?

Tell us what happened, which device and operating system you're using, and the app version if you know it. A screenshot can help, but please leave out passwords and other private information.
